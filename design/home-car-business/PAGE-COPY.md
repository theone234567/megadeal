## Real offer ideas for your kind of business.

Different businesses need different promotions. Start with an idea below, then shape the scope and price around your own business.


### Cleaning {#cleaning}

Give new customers a clear first step into your cleaning service. A focused package can introduce your business while keeping the work, property size and service area manageable.

**Example offer ideas**

- **First-home-clean package** — Introduce your business with a defined clean for new customers.
- **Midweek oven clean** — Promote oven cleaning on selected quieter weekdays.
- **Carpet + upholstery bundle** — Combine selected carpet areas with a sofa or chair clean.
- **End-of-tenancy clean** — Offer a clear cleaning package for people moving out.

**Make the scope clear:** Specify hours, rooms, tasks, supplies and any property-size limit. State oven type, size and whether racks or additional appliances are included. Define room sizes, seat count, fabric exclusions and extra stain-treatment charges. Define property size and inclusions; do not guarantee a bond refund.

### Garden & Outdoors {#garden-outdoors}

Help local homeowners get on top of their outdoor jobs. Promote a well-defined service in suburbs you already cover, with enough detail to manage travel time, access and workload.

**Example offer ideas**

- **Lawn mow + edges** — Bundle mowing and edge trimming for a tidy finish.
- **Seasonal garden tidy** — Offer a fixed block of time for selected garden tasks.
- **Hedge-trimming package** — Promote trimming for suitable hedges in your service area.
- **Driveway water blasting** — Promote cleaning for eligible driveways or paved areas.

**Make the scope clear:** Set a lawn-area limit and explain slope, access and overgrown-lawn conditions. List included tasks, labour hours and whether green-waste removal is included. Specify length, height, access and waste disposal; quote larger work separately. Specify surface type and area; assess suitability and explain water/access requirements.

### Home Maintenance {#home-maintenance}

Show local homeowners which jobs your business can help with. Specific, manageable packages make it easier to understand the work and request the right service.

**Example offer ideas**

- **Single-storey gutter clean** — Promote a gutter clean for eligible homes.
- **Window-cleaning package** — Bundle selected inside or outside windows.
- **Small handyman job bundle** — Offer a time allowance for a defined list of small tasks.
- **Seasonal home check** — Offer a visual check with a written list of maintenance observations.

**Make the scope clear:** Define gutter length, safe-access requirements, downpipe scope and disposal. State pane count, storeys, access and whether tracks or screens are included. Specify eligible work, labour, materials and exclusions; preserve applicable trade requirements. Describe its scope accurately; do not call it a certified inspection unless that is the actual qualified service.

### Car Wash & Detailing {#car-wash-detailing}

Turn spare wash-bay or mobile-detailing capacity into a reason to try your business. Make each package easy to compare by clearly describing the finish and vehicle coverage.

**Example offer ideas**

- **Midweek full valet** — Promote an interior and exterior package on selected weekdays.
- **Interior deep clean** — Offer a focused clean for seats, carpets and interior surfaces.
- **Wash + wax package** — Combine an exterior wash with a defined protective finish.
- **Two-car household offer** — Bundle eligible vehicles at one location or booking.

**Make the scope clear:** List every included task, vehicle sizes, booking rules and extra-soiling charges. Specify the methods and inclusions; explain pet-hair, stain and odour exclusions. Name what is applied and the coverage; avoid unsupported durability claims. Define vehicle eligibility, location, access and how the package price is calculated.

### Servicing & Repairs {#servicing-repairs}

Give drivers a clear reason to discover your workshop. Promote services you can price and deliver consistently, with vehicle eligibility and possible extra work explained upfront.

**Example offer ideas**

- **Oil + filter package** — Promote an oil and filter change for selected vehicles.
- **Off-peak service booking** — Offer selected service packages during quieter workshop periods.
- **Battery check package** — Introduce a battery testing service with clearly listed checks.
- **Diagnostic assessment** — Offer a defined initial diagnostic assessment.

**Make the scope clear:** Specify eligible vehicles, oil specification and quantity, filter, labour and extra charges. Name the service scope and available days; do not imply live availability without integration. State what the test covers and whether replacement parts or fitting cost extra. Set the time allowance and report scope; assessment does not guarantee a diagnosis or repair.

### Tyres & Wheels {#tyres-wheels}

Introduce drivers to your tyre and wheel services with a specific, easy-to-understand offer. Clear vehicle and equipment requirements help customers choose an appropriate booking.

**Example offer ideas**

- **Wheel alignment** — Promote an alignment for eligible vehicles.
- **Rotation + balancing** — Bundle tyre rotation with balancing for eligible wheels.
- **Selected tyre package** — Create a package for named tyre sizes or ranges.
- **Puncture assessment** — Offer an assessment of a damaged tyre.

**Make the scope clear:** State two-wheel or four-wheel scope and any adjustments, exclusions or extra work. Specify wheel count, sizes, compatibility and exclusions. Specify products, quantity, fitting, balancing, disposal, availability and total applicable charges. Repair is subject to suitability; show assessment and repair charges separately when applicable.

## Make your offer clear from the start.

A useful offer answers the customer's practical questions before they book.

- **Service area:** Name the suburbs or areas you cover and any travel or call-out charges.
- **What's included:** Define the tasks, time allowance, materials, vehicle or property eligibility, and exclusions.
- **When it applies:** State booking requirements, valid dates and any restricted days or times.
- **Extra charges:** Explain what costs extra and confirm additional work before carrying it out.

### Start with one focused offer.

Choose a service you deliver well and can describe clearly. A midweek valet, a lawn-and-edge bundle or an introductory home clean can be easier to understand than a broad discount across everything you do. Check the labour, materials and travel involved before setting your offer.
